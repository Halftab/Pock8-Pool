Pass & Play

Taking turns

Take turns shooting. Pocketing one of your assigned balls lets you shoot again.

Solids & stripes

The first object ball pocketed assigns solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15). Pocket your group, then the 8-ball.

Fouls

If the cue ball is pocketed, your turn ends and the opponent may place the cue ball anywhere on the table.

The 8-ball

After clearing your group, pocket the 8-ball to win. If the 8-ball is pocketed before you clear all your assigned balls, your opponent wins.
